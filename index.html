🚨 Critical Finding: Facility Level Effect

Health Posts have a 57.5% stockout rate vs only 4.3% at National level — a 13× gap proving that the last-mile is where the supply chain breaks down (Kruskal-Wallis H=1167.6, p<0.0001).

📅 Seasonal Pattern Confirmed

Stockout rates peak at 53.9% in March and fall to 35.9% in September — a statistically significant 18-point seasonal swing (Friedman χ²=199.8, p<0.0001). Likely linked to rainy season logistics disruption.

💰 GDP is the Key Predictor

Spearman r = −0.465 (p=0.039): wealthier countries stock out less. Notably, health spending % GDP alone was NOT significant, suggesting it's overall economic capacity — not just allocation — that drives supply chain performance.

🤖 ML Risk Score Deployed

Random Forest (200 trees, 5-fold CV) achieves AUC=0.662. Facility level and month of year are the two dominant predictors (combined 80% feature importance), enabling targeted interventions before stockouts occur.

ROC-AUC: 0.662 — meaningful predictive signal

Stable across all 5 folds (low std deviation)

🔍 What the Model Tells Us

Facility level (61%) is by far the dominant predictor — lower-tier facilities are structurally more likely to stock out regardless of other factors. Seasonality (19%) is second, confirming the Q1 peak. GDP and health spending contribute only ~2% each.

🚀 Real-World Application

Deploy this model as an early warning system: input current facility level, month, stock receipt history, and country GDP → output stockout probability. Flag High/Critical facilities for emergency resupply 4–6 weeks ahead of predicted stockout.

H1: Facility Level Effect ✅ CONFIRMED

Kruskal-Wallis H = 1167.6, p < 0.0001. All 15 pairwise comparisons between facility levels are significant. The hierarchy matters enormously — national facilities are nearly stockout-free while health posts exceed 57%.

H2: GDP Correlation ✅ SIGNIFICANT

Spearman r = −0.465, p = 0.039. Wealthier countries have better-stocked facilities. Health spending % GDP alone (r=−0.350, p=0.13) is NOT significant — it's total economic capacity that matters, not just health budget allocation.

H3: Seasonal Variation ✅ CONFIRMED

Friedman χ² = 199.8, p < 0.0001. Stockout rates vary significantly by month across all countries. Peak in March (53.9%), trough in September (35.9%) — a 18-point seasonal range with clear rainy-season signal.

H4: Facility Class vs Level ✅ CONFIRMED

Chi-square χ² = 1563.7, df=10, p < 0.0001. Facility level and chronic-stockout classification are strongly associated — lower-level facilities are systematically more likely to be classified as "Chronic" stockout sites.
