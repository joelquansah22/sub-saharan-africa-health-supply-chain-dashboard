01 — Key Findings
What the Data Reveals
Four statistically validated findings with direct policy implications

🚨 13× Gap Between Facility Levels

Health Posts stockout at 57.5% while National facilities sit at 4.3%. The last mile is where the supply chain collapses. Kruskal-Wallis H=1167.6, p<0.0001 — all 15 pairwise comparisons significant.

📅 18-Point Seasonal Swing Every Year

Stockouts peak at 53.9% in March and fall to 35.9% in September. Friedman χ²=199.8, p<0.0001. The Q1 rainy season disrupts logistics — a predictable pattern that could be pre-empted.

💰 GDP, Not Health Spending, Is the Real Driver

GDP per capita: r=−0.465, p=0.039 (significant). Health spending % GDP: r=−0.350, p=0.130 (not significant). Overall economic capacity matters more than budget allocation — a finding that challenges standard policy logic.

🤖 Facility Level Explains 61% of ML Predictive Power

Random Forest (200 trees, 5-fold CV) achieves AUC=0.662. Facility level alone accounts for 61.3% of feature importance. Month of year adds 18.8%. This means structural factors dominate over country-level interventions.

📈 Interpretation for Policy

Because facility level (61%) and month (19%) dominate predictions, the most cost-effective intervention is targeted emergency resupply to peripheral facilities in Q1. Country-level GDP and spending (combined ~2%) are too distal to act upon operationally.
